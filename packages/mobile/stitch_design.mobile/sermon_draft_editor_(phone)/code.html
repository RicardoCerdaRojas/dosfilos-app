"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God..." — Ephesians 2:8

Introduction: The Unearned Gift

Imagine receiving a package at your door. There's no return address, no invoice, and certainly no bill. Inside is exactly what you needed, but never thought you could afford. This is the essence of grace. In our study today, we'll examine how this theological concept translates into our daily walk.

I. The Anatomy of Grace

Grace isn't just a sentiment; it's a divine action. It is God reaching down when we were unable to reach up. Many of us struggle with the idea of 'free.' We think there must be a catch. But the Gospel is clear: the price was paid by another.

When we look at the Greek word charis, we find a beauty that transcends simple "favor." It implies a joyful gift that brings delight to both the giver and the receiver.

How do we respond to such a gift?
