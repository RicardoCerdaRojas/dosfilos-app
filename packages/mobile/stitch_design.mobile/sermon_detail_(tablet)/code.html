Main Passage

Romans 1:16-17

NRSVUE • Preached on Oct 24, 2024

Speaker

Dr. Elias Thorne

Theological Professor

The Big Idea

"The Gospel is not just a message to be believed, but a dynamic power from God that actively transforms and justifies those who live by faith."

Introduction

Open with the historical context of Rome in the first century. Contrast the earthly power of the Roman Empire with the spiritual power of the Kingdom of God. Use the illustration of the "Roman Road" as a metaphor for the spread of ideas.

POINT I

Unashamed of the Gospel

Exegesis v. 16a
"For I am not ashamed of the gospel; it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who has faith, to the Jew first and also to the Greek."
  • fiber_manual_record

    The Greek word dynamis (power) suggests an active, explosive force, not just potential.

  • fiber_manual_record

    Paul’s lack of shame is a direct challenge to the social honor/shame culture of Rome.

  • fiber_manual_record

    Illustration: The Surgeon

    A surgeon isn't ashamed of a scalpel, even though it cuts. It is the power that brings healing to the dying patient.

POINT II

The Righteousness of God Revealed

Theology v. 17

Discuss the concept of dikaiosynē theou. Is it an attribute of God or a gift from God? For Luther, this was the "gateway to paradise." We must emphasize that this righteousness is revealed "from faith to faith."

history_edu Cross-Reference

Habakkuk 2:4 — "The righteous shall live by his faith." Compare Paul's use of this minor prophet.

psychology Thought Provoker

How does the modern world define "righteousness" vs. how Paul defines it here?

Conclusion & Call to Action

Application Points:

  1. 1

    Identify one area of your life where you have been "ashamed" of your convictions.

  2. 2

    Commit to a specific act of "faith-living" this week—trusting God's power over your own ability.

"Closing Prayer: Asking for the Spirit to activate this 'power' in our community."
