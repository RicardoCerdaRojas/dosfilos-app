Justification

The act of God's free grace, wherein he pardoneth all our sins, and accepteth us as righteous in his sight.

In Christian theology, justification is God's righteous act of removing the guilt and penalty of sin while, at the same time, declaring a sinner righteous through Christ's atoning sacrifice. In Protestantism, it is often summarized as "Sola Fide" (by faith alone).
The doctrine was the central concern of the 16th-century Protestant Reformation. Martin Luther famously described it as the "article upon which the church stands or falls." It distinguishes the Protestant view (imputed righteousness) from the Roman Catholic view (infused righteousness).
