Ephesians 2:1-10

Made Alive in Christ

"And you were dead in the trespasses and sins in which you once walked, following the course of this world..."

The transition from death to life is the core of Paul's message here. In the original Greek, the word νεκροὺς (nekrous) emphasizes a total inability to respond spiritually.

Sermon Point 1: The condition of humanity apart from intervention. Focus on the word 'grace' which appears as the catalyst for transformation in verse 5.
