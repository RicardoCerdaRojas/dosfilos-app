Theology of Grace

Section I

The Scandal of Unmerited Favor

In the ancient world, the concept of charis was not merely a word for "grace," but a social contract of reciprocity. If I do for you, you must do for me.

But when we look at the Pauline literature, we see a radical subversion of this economy. Grace is described as an asymmetrical gift—a favor given to those who could never, under any circumstances, repay the giver.

"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast."

— Ephesians 2:8-9 (ESV)
Section II
